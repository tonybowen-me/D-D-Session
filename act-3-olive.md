# Act 3: The Unmaking at Brackenmill

**Run time:** 50–65 minutes  
**Mode:** full-party rescue, machine operation, resonance, and hard choices; no combat  
**Location:** Pell Arkwright's hut in Brackenmill, an inland town many days and very far from Tidewick

## Premise and boundaries

Olive sits awake inside the enormous **Concordance Engine**, assembled from Gannet Nine docked into the hut's flywheel, pipes, and control gantry. Supernatural puppeteer twine seals her eyes and mouth. The twine belongs to **the Bailiff of Strings**, a hell-contract bounty hunter and puppet master who took Olive's parents. Why he took them is unknown. This act gives no answer, inference, or secretly definitive explanation.

The **Resonant Hearthstone** can make the twine tangible enough for the Engine to unwind it. The process is dangerous because the twine reacts to forced control, but Olive is a participant, not an object: she chooses whether to begin, communicates throughout, can call for pauses or an abort, and makes the final decision in every hard choice involving her.

Describe constriction, heat, vibration, exhaustion, and threads pulling taut—not torn flesh. The stitches are supernatural loops lying against the skin. On success they dissolve or lift free without gore.

## Arrival from Tidewick

Use the campaign's established fast travel if one exists. Otherwise, Pell activates **Gannet's Hearthline Return**: the repaired machine follows the Hearthstone's home-bearing across the great distance to its workshop in a compressed travel montage. Sela's ceramic collar makes the passage smooth; without it, Pell improvises a safe collar over several additional hours and sacrifices Gannet's message-lamp or diagnostic lens. Tidewick remains useful, never mandatory.

> The world beyond Gannet's amber glass becomes a sequence of held notes: salt wind, dark fields, rain striking iron, then cedar smoke. With every pulse of the Hearthstone, the same distant workshop lamp appears closer. At last the machine's feet strike the Brackenmill road, and the stone in your hands answers a second rhythm from inside Pell's hut—faint, deliberate, alive.

Cut directly to the door. Ask one player what their character carried from Tidewick and another what promise they rehearsed for Olive during the passage.

## Safety and calibration

Before play, say plainly:

> “This scene involves a child in peril, magically sewn-shut eyes and mouth, loss of bodily control, and a remote possibility of death after repeated, clearly warned failures. It contains no graphic injury. We can veil the stitches, remove death, pause, rewind, or replace the imagery with a binding mask at any time.”

Confirm lines/veils and the table's pause tool. Ask whether players want:

- **Standard stakes:** death is possible only through the Catastrophe procedure below.
- **Rescue stakes:** replace death with coma, planar displacement, or permanent loss of the Hearthstone.
- **Veiled description:** speak only of “bindings” and machine readings.

Never use Olive's distress to punish a player for setting a boundary. Do not imitate choking, force a player to portray panic, or make Olive's consent a trick. If the table aborts, Olive survives.

## Cast and communication

### Olive

Olive is frightened, observant, stubborn, and capable of making choices. Establish a code before activation:

- one squeeze/tap: **yes**
- two: **no**
- three rapid: **stop now**
- held squeeze: **I trust you / stay with me**
- traced letters, *message*, *sending*, telepathy, familiar contact, or Hearthstone resonance can carry fuller thoughts

When the Hearthstone reaches Resonance 2+, Olive can communicate mentally in short sensory phrases to any PC touching a console: *warm kitchen; left coil wrong; I can hold; not yet; now.*

Olive may refuse activation. She may later ask to continue, pause, or abort. A PC can help her understand but cannot roll to override her decision.

### The Tinkerer

**Pell “Pell” Arkwright**, soot-streaked, direct, and gentle, turns his hut and repaired mecha-machine Gannet Nine into the Concordance Engine: mill flywheel, organ pipes, glass capacitors, and an iron cradle.

Useful lines:

- “She is not a component. Ask her before you touch a lever.”
- “The Hearthstone finds a true note. We make the machine humble enough to follow it.”
- “Red means danger. Black means we have one choice left.”
- “Do not promise it won't hurt. Promise she won't face it alone.”
- On creative magic: “If it carries rhythm, steadies heat, reveals tension, or gives her a voice, wire it in.”
- On the parents: “I can tell you whose craft this is. I cannot tell you why he chose them—and I won't sell you a guess as truth.”
- Before catastrophe: “Hands off the pride. Save the child.”

## Scene clock

| Segment | Time | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Arrival and calibration | 7–10 min | establish distance, consent, communication, and roles |
| Survey and preparation | 8–10 min | earn setup advantages and read the machine |
| Engine sequence | 25–35 min | 4–6 visible rounds |
| Release and aftermath | 10–12 min | outcome, recovery, clues, choices |

To keep within 50 minutes, use 4 rounds and one aftermath scene. For 60–65, use 5 rounds, allow a repair interlude, and run all aftermath beats.

## Visible setup

Place these tracks where everyone can see them:

### Core tracks

- **Resonance:** 0–6; start 0. At 6, release is possible.
- **Strain:** 0–6; start 0. At 4, Warning; at 6, Catastrophe begins.
- **Olive's Trust:** 0–3; start 1 (or 2 if the party consistently respected her agency). Trust is spent only with Olive's indicated consent.
- **Rounds:** 1–5. Use 4 rounds for parties of 2–3 PCs, 5 for 4–6 PCs, 6 for 7+ PCs.
- **Catastrophe:** Safe → Warning → Redline → Blackout → Severance.

### Success target

At the end of the final normal round:

- **Release:** Resonance 6.
- **Stable release:** Resonance 6 and Strain 3 or lower.
- If Resonance is below 6, proceed to fail-forward outcomes; do not keep rolling indefinitely.

## Scaling

Use the party's **average level**, rounded down:

| Average level | Standard DC | Hard DC | Desperate DC |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1–4 | 13 | 15 | 17 |
| 5–8 | 14 | 16 | 18 |
| 9–12 | 15 | 17 | 19 |
| 13–16 | 16 | 18 | 20 |
| 17–20 | 17 | 19 | 21 |

Party-size adjustments:

- **2–3 PCs:** 4 rounds; each PC may take one primary action and one different support action per round; 5 Resonance is enough to release.
- **4–6 PCs:** 5 rounds; each PC takes one action per round; standard rules.
- **7+ PCs:** 6 rounds; split into 3–4 simultaneous stations; two different PCs may contribute to the same station, but only one rolls and the other grants a setup benefit.

Do not increase DCs for a small party. For a very optimized party, add hard choices, not inflated DCs.

## Tidewick-earned Favors and prizes

The party can bring at most **3 Tidewick Favors**, as established in Act 2. Cross off one Favor to:

- gain advantage on a check involving rescue, ropes, contracts, acoustic vibration, navigation, hulls, or community coordination;
- declare one packed mundane rescue supply worth 2 gp or less;
- ask for one concrete, truthful craft or environmental detail;
- turn a failed noncombat group check into a mixed success (+1 Resonance/+1 Strain here);
- establish a plausible friendly Tidewick contact by message.

Specific Act 2 prizes also matter once each:

| Prize | Engine use |
|---|---|
| **Fen's frequency interval** | Set one failed Hearthkeeper tuning check to the Standard DC without adding Strain. |
| **Rescue line or Ropewalk Favor** | Prevent 1 Strain from a Flywheel or bracing consequence. |
| **Listening cup or twine sample** | Gain an Insight token and identify the safer of two presented sequences. |
| **Witness-knot diagram** | Give a Thread Reader advantage and authenticate one contract clue. |
| **Rescuer's packet or Bakehouse Favor** | Honor a pause without losing Resonance; Olive or one PC clears exhaustion enough to continue. |
| **Trained rescue assistance** | A helper stabilizes one station, removing 1 Strain; only if geographically plausible or represented by prior preparation. |

If the party skipped Tidewick, equivalent benefits remain available through Act 3 checks and resources. A sentimental Tidewick keepsake may grant advantage once when used to comfort Olive.

## Survey and preparation

Give every PC one preparation contribution. A preparation skill cannot be repeated unless the approach and fictional target differ.

| Approach | DC | Success | Failure |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Arcana or Religion: read infernal resonance without probing motive | Standard | learn one clue and place 1 **Insight token** | learn the clue, but +1 Strain when the Engine starts |
| Investigation or Tinker's tools: map controls | Standard | place 1 **Spare token** | identify a cracked relay; it becomes a hard choice in round 2 |
| Medicine: establish Olive's limits and stop signal | Standard | +1 Trust (max 3) | no loss; Pell supplies the baseline |
| Insight, Performance, or Persuasion: build a communication rhythm | Standard | +1 Trust or 1 **Cadence token** | Olive corrects the PC; gain the code with no token |
| Carpenter's, smith's, glassblower's, or thieves' tools: brace a station | Hard | prevent the first Strain generated there | the flaw is exposed safely; next check there is Hard |
| Relevant spell used creatively | usually no roll if exact; otherwise Standard spellcasting check | gain the matching token or preparation benefit | spell still reveals useful information; no slot refund |

**Tokens:** Insight reveals a consequence before committing; Spare rerolls a machine check but must keep the new result; Cadence gives advantage to communication/resonance. Unspent preparation tokens carry into the Engine.

## Meaningful stations

Each round, every PC chooses a station or proposes an equivalent job. Encourage rotation.

1. **Hearthkeeper:** tunes the Resonant Hearthstone (Arcana, Religion, Performance, musical instrument).
2. **Flywheel Engineer:** controls speed and torque (Athletics, Tinker's/smith's/carpenter's tools, *mending*, controlled force).
3. **Thread Reader:** identifies which loop can safely release (Investigation, Perception, Medicine, divination).
4. **Grounder:** bleeds heat and infernal feedback (Survival, Nature, alchemist's/glassblower's tools, cold/lightning/abjuration used carefully).
5. **Anchor:** communicates with Olive and protects her choices (Insight, Medicine, Persuasion, telepathy, *message*, calming or warding magic).
6. **Conductor:** coordinates timing (Performance, History of mechanisms, group toolwork, a clear plan).

Any sensible skill, tool, class feature, or spell works if the player explains what it changes. Exact-counter spells may succeed without a roll but still consume their resource. Magic cannot simply dispel the contract twine; it can reveal, steady, communicate, insulate, or power the Engine.

## Round procedure

Do not roll combat initiative. Go around the table in whichever order players choose each round.

1. **Announce the pulse.** Read the round prompt and show the hard choice.
2. **Hear Olive.** Give one squeeze/tap/resonance message. Players may ask one yes/no question for free.
3. **Choose roles.** Every PC states a distinct contribution.
4. **Resolve actions.** Usually Standard DC; use Hard when rushed, damaged, or opposing the pulse. Record effects immediately.
5. **Reckon.** Apply the round's hard choice, narrate the current state, and mark the round.

### Check results

| Result | Effect |
|---|---|
| Beat DC by 5+ or natural 20 | +2 Resonance, or +1 Resonance and remove 1 Strain |
| Meet DC | +1 Resonance |
| Miss by 1–4 | useful mixed success: +1 Resonance and +1 Strain, **or** no Resonance and create a token; acting player chooses |
| Miss by 5+ or natural 1 | no Resonance, +1 Strain, and reveal a concrete warning; never instant catastrophe |

Only the first **two** successful primary actions per round add Resonance (one for parties of 2–3; three for 7+). Other successes remove Strain, gain/restore Trust, create tokens, or cancel complications. This keeps every role meaningful without making party size an automatic win.

### Anti-spam

After a PC uses the same skill, spell, or tool in the same way once, repeating it is **Hard**. A third identical use cannot add Resonance; it can only support another PC or mitigate Strain. Reset this restriction when the fictional problem changes substantially.

## The Engine sequence

### Round 1: Find Her Note

**Pulse:** organ pipes answer Olive's heartbeat while the Hearthstone searches for her chosen note.

**Olive:** one tap, then a slow rhythm: *ready, but listen.*

**Hard choice:**  
**Gentle start:** all checks are Standard, but the round can add at most 1 Resonance.  
**Fast lock:** resonance actions have advantage and can add normally; any failed check adds +1 additional Strain.

**Sample narration by state:**

- Safe: “Amber light moves through the pipes in time with Olive's fingers.”
- Warning: “One note arrives sharp enough to rattle every tool; Pell paints a chalk line at Strain 4.”
- Redline: “The flywheel skips. Olive gives three rapid taps. Every gauge points toward the abort lever.”

### Round 2: The False Harmony

**Pulse:** the infernal twine mimics a trusted voice. Never identify whose voice it copies unless Olive volunteers it.

**Olive:** *Not them. Wrong warmth.* She squeezes twice.

**Hard choice:**  
**Mute the echo:** spend 1 Resonance; all Anchor and Thread Reader checks gain advantage.  
**Work through it:** keep Resonance; Anchor is Hard. On an Anchor failure, +1 Strain but Olive still exposes the false note.

If the preparation relay was cracked, someone must repair it at Standard DC or accept +1 Strain. The repair still succeeds on failure; the cost is heat and delay.

**Sample narration:**

- Safe: “Your call-and-response drowns the imitation without ever asking Olive to relive it.”
- Warning: “The false voice finds the pipes. Olive's real rhythm remains underneath—quiet, deliberate, hers.”
- Redline: “Glass whitens around the edges. Pell says, ‘That is mimicry, not consent. Follow her hand.’”

### Round 3: Contract Hook

**Pulse:** a barbed clause of resonance catches the Hearthstone. It shows legal texture but no motive.

**Olive:** *It pulls when you decide for me.*

**Hard choice:** ask Olive:

- **Let the hook remain:** next round begins at Hard for Hearthkeeper; gain 1 Insight token and preserve the Hearthstone.
- **Burn it out:** remove the hook and +1 Strain; the Hearthstone permanently loses one future charge/major use.
- **Share the burden:** a willing PC accepts a temporary resonance scar (disadvantage on their next two Wisdom saves after the act); remove 1 Strain. This is sacrifice, not damage, and needs explicit player consent.

**Sample narration:**

- Safe: “The Hearthstone shows a knot shaped like a signature, then waits for Olive's tap before loosening.”
- Warning: “Letters without words crawl across the gauge. They reveal enforcement, not purpose.”
- Redline: “The hook turns toward Olive whenever anyone speaks over her. It eases when the room waits.”

### Round 4: The Unspooling

**Pulse:** the first loops lift. The Engine needs perfect sequence, not force.

**Olive:** a held squeeze: *Keep going.* If Trust is 0, she instead requests a pause; honoring it removes 1 Strain and uses no round.

**Hard choice:**  
**Protect sight:** prioritize eye loops; mouth remains bound into aftermath, but remove 1 Strain.  
**Restore voice:** prioritize mouth loops; Olive can verbally coordinate the finale, granting one automatic Standard success, but +1 Strain as the eye loops tighten temporarily.  
Olive makes the choice after the consequences are explained.

**Sample narration:**

- Safe: “One dark loop rises like thread from water and becomes harmless ash.”
- Warning: “The Engine has the sequence, but not the patience; the brake begins to glow.”
- Redline: “The whole hut hums in Olive's note. The next mistake will not be hidden.”

### Round 5: Release

Skip this round for a short session; resolve after round 4. For 7+ PCs, add a sixth stabilization round using the same structure.

**Pulse:** the twine, Hearthstone, and machine align. State the current tracks and likely outcome before choices.

**Olive:** *Now. Together.* Or, if she wants out, three rapid taps; abort immediately.

Each PC describes one final contribution. Make at most three rolls total: one machine, one resonance, one communication. Other PCs support or pay costs. Standard if Strain 0–3, Hard if 4–5, Desperate if 6/Catastrophe.

**Final hard choice if Resonance is short by 1:**

- spend 2 Trust with Olive's consent to reach 6;
- crack the Hearthstone, reaching 6 but destroying it;
- accept partial release and preserve both Olive and the Hearthstone.

Never offer a lethal gamble here unless Catastrophe has already progressed through its explicit procedure.

## Catastrophe procedure

Strain 6 does **not** kill Olive. It begins a visible, multi-step emergency. Say each state name aloud, explain the next consequence, and provide an exit.

### 1. Warning (Strain 4–5)

> “The amber lamps turn red. Olive's rhythm stutters, then deliberately resumes. Pell says: ‘One more surge starts an emergency. We can stop safely now.’”

Players may pause: lose 1 Resonance and remove 2 Strain. They may abort with a partial-release outcome. A failed check cannot move more than one catastrophe step.

### 2. Redline (Strain 6)

> “The brake screams and the Hearthstone shows a black fracture. No one is dying now—but another uncontained surge will cause Blackout. The abort lever is unlocked.”

Normal progress stops. The party must choose:

- **Abort:** Olive lives; use **Partial release**.
- **Stabilize:** complete a group challenge: 2 successes before 2 failures at Hard DC, using distinct approaches. Success returns to Warning at Strain 4 and costs 1 Resonance. Failure advances to Blackout.
- **Sacrifice equipment:** destroy a major magic item or the Hearthstone to return to Warning at Strain 3 automatically. The owning player must consent.

### 3. Blackout

> “The machine goes dark. In the silence, Olive gives three rapid taps. Pell's hand closes over the final disconnect: ‘The next restart could sever her resonance. Abort and she lives. Restart only if everyone—including her—chooses it.’”

This is the mandatory final abort/sacrifice choice:

- **Final abort:** Olive lives; the twine remains partly attached and she needs future care.
- **Pell's sacrifice:** Pell destroys the Engine and takes the backlash; Olive lives, Pell survives but permanently loses the use of one hand (or another non-gory consequence agreed by the table).
- **Hearthstone sacrifice:** destroy it; Olive lives and all twine releases, but the future benefit is gone.
- **Restart:** permitted only after the DM restates that death is now possible, all players affirm the chosen stakes, and Olive clearly consents. It requires 3 successes before 2 failures at Desperate DC, all with distinct skills/tools and no advantage from routine Help.

On restart success, Olive survives with a costly release. On the first failure, narrate a final warning and offer abort/sacrifice again. Only a **second** failure after all prior warnings advances to Severance.

### 4. Severance (possible death)

> “The Hearthstone's note breaks. There is still one instant to throw the disconnect, but doing so demands a willing sacrifice.”

Offer one last choice:

- a willing PC permanently gives up a treasured magic item, major supernatural boon, or agreed piece of their own resonance; Olive lives;
- Pell gives up his life's work and future craft; Olive lives;
- if everyone declines and the table retained Standard stakes, Olive dies quietly as her resonance is severed.

No death roll is made. Veil the moment:

> “Her hand gives one final, gentle squeeze. The machine falls silent.”

Death requires Strain 6, failed stabilization, chosen restart, two Desperate failures with repeated warnings, and refusal of final abort/sacrifice. Any earlier abort means survival.

## Outcome tiers

| Tier | Requirements | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Perfect concordance** | Resonance 6; Strain 0–2; Trust 2–3 | all twine removed; Olive alert and empowered; Hearthstone and Engine intact; gain two clues and **Olive's Counter-Rhythm** boon |
| **Clean release** | Resonance 6; Strain 3–5 | all twine removed; Olive needs rest; equipment mostly intact; gain two clues |
| **Costly release** | release after Redline/Blackout or a sacrifice | Olive survives and twine is removed; apply the chosen lasting cost; gain one clue |
| **Partial release** | Resonance 3–5 or safe abort | eyes or mouth freed by Olive's choice; remaining twine is dormant; future procedure possible; gain one clue |
| **Protected failure** | Resonance 0–2 or early abort | Olive survives unchanged, now able to communicate through the Hearthstone at short range; party learns what the next attempt needs |
| **Death** | Severance procedure completed and all saves/sacrifices declined | Olive dies without graphic description; the failure changes relationships and future goals, never provides secret motive information |

**Olive's Counter-Rhythm:** once in a future noncombat scene involving coercive magic, the party may invoke Olive's learned rhythm to grant everyone advantage on one coordinated resistance check or automatically establish communication with a bound creature.

## Aftermath

### First breath (3–4 minutes)

Let Olive control the first contact. Pell asks, “Touch, water, quiet, or questions?” Olive chooses. Do not make her immediately explain trauma.

Sample Olive lines after full release:

- “I heard you every time you asked.”
- “He wanted me still. I wasn't.”
- “My parents were alive when he took them. I don't know why.”

On partial release, use taps, writing, or mental resonance. Celebrate what was restored without framing Olive as incomplete.

### The parents (2–3 minutes)

If asked why the Bailiff took them, Olive and Pell do not know. Honest answers:

- Olive: “He read something from a page I couldn't see. He never said why.”
- Pell: “A bounty clause tells us someone claimed a debt. It does not tell us what the claimant truly wants.”

Do not turn a high check into the motive. A successful check distinguishes fact from speculation.

### Choice of next step (3–5 minutes)

Offer Olive options: remain with Pell to recover, travel with trusted guardians, or help study the safe fragments. She expresses a preference; adults discuss logistics without erasing it.

### Quiet coda (optional, 2 minutes)

Each PC names one sound, gesture, or object that helped Olive remain present. Olive adds one of her own. End on agency rather than injury.

## Mystery-preserving clues

Award clues according to outcome. These are true but do not answer motive:

1. **Infernal maker's mark:** the twine carries the Bailiff of Strings' seal and an enforcement number, proving authorship, not who hired him.
2. **Transit resonance:** grit in a loop resonates with three distant thresholds—salt, cedar smoke, and cold iron. They are route fragments, not a destination.
3. **Redacted clause:** the Engine briefly reveals: “DELIVER THE TWO SIGNATORIES—PURPOSE SEALED BY SUPERIOR ARTICLE.” The superior article cannot be recovered here.
4. **Counterfeit warmth:** the twine can mimic familiar voices from surface memories; this explains its tactic, not its goal.
5. **Missing strand:** one thread was removed before Olive arrived, suggesting another use or recipient. Its purpose remains unknown.

Checks can authenticate, date, or compare these clues. They cannot reveal the sealed superior article, employer, destination, or reason for taking the parents.

## DM adjudication examples

- **“I cast *mending* on the cracked relay.”** It is an exact fit: the relay is repaired, no roll, slot spent; gain a Spare token if the character also has relevant tool proficiency.
- **“I use *dispel magic* on the twine.”** The contract regenerates, but the spell exposes its rhythm. Spellcasting ability check at Hard DC: success grants +1 Resonance; failure grants an Insight token and +1 Strain.
- **“I rage and hold the flywheel.”** Athletics at Standard DC, with advantage if rage plausibly applies. Success prevents 1 Strain and lets another PC tune safely; raw force alone does not remove twine.
- **“My familiar relays Olive's taps.”** No roll to establish communication. If used to predict a pulse, Perception at Standard DC for a Cadence token.
- **“I keep using Guidance.”** It can enhance different characters normally, but does not count as a distinct station contribution and cannot generate Resonance by itself.
- **“Can I roll Persuasion to make Olive continue?”** No. Explain risks and ask her. Persuasion may help communicate clearly, never replace consent.

## DM checklist

- Put all tracks in view and announce every change.
- Give Olive a communication beat every round.
- Make consequences known before each hard choice.
- Let every PC contribute with a distinct fictional method.
- Reward Tidewick history and expended resources.
- Treat failed rolls as costs, information, or altered outcomes—not dead ends.
- Preserve the unknown motive.
- Trigger death only through the full Catastrophe procedure.
- Stop or veil immediately when requested.
