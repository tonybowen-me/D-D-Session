# Background Events: Three-Act Clock

This tracker runs behind all three acts whether the party investigates, rests, travels, splits up, or ignores a lead. It creates motion and consequences without inventing the hell-contract puppet master's motive or forcing a specific route.

## Fixed Continuity

- Act 1 begins immediately after the cave recovery and boss fight.
- The party and Pell “Pell” Arkwright are on the beach. Gannet Nine lies on its back in shallow water while Pell works its controls.
- The Resonant Hearthstone pulses in the sand.
- Olive is **not in Tidewick**. She is very far away at Pell's hut in **Brackenmill**, a different town.
- Olive's eyes and mouth have been sewn shut with supernatural twine by a hell-contract puppet master.
- The twine is active coercive contract-work. Its exact rules, author, current location, goals, and reason for targeting Olive are **unknown**.
- Do not convert an omen, rumor, inference, or NPC theory into an answer about motive.
- The session is zero-combat. Escalation creates time pressure, social complications, harder choices, and worsening signs—not attacks or fake combat.

## How to Run the Clock

Use six segments across three acts. Advance one segment:

- at the close of each major scene;
- after a short rest or equivalent delay;
- when the party spends substantial time repeating an exhausted approach;
- when a listed escalation trigger occurs.

Do **not** advance it for normal questions, thoughtful roleplay, one failed check, accessibility needs, or reasonable planning. Tell players about time through fiction before imposing consequences.

The clock is not a death timer. At segment 6, Olive is in grave and worsening distress but remains available to the continuing story unless the campaign has separately established otherwise.

### Pacing adjustment

If Act 1 runs only 25 minutes, end it at segment 2. If it runs 35 minutes with a short rest or extended repair, segment 3 may begin at the transition to Act 2. Never jump two segments merely because players are cautious.

## Act-by-Act Background Flow

## Act 1: Beach, Machine, and First Bearing

### Automatic events

1. **Mara notices:** Mara Venn sees Gannet Nine's steam from her fishing path and watches openly from the dune.
2. **Tide rises:** Water gradually reaches the machine's vulnerable hip housing in about ninety minutes, but a basic stabilization or moving it a few yards removes this pressure.
3. **Stone aligns:** The Hearthstone's pulse gains an inland lean. It does not point to Tidewick as a destination; its bearing passes beyond it toward distant Brackenmill.
4. **Olive answers indirectly:** The first time her name is spoken near the stone, the pulse stutters and answers twice. This may be reflex, resonance, or conscious effort; do not decide yet.

### If the party does nothing

- Pell completes basic stabilization in ten minutes.
- Mara eventually approaches to offer Tidewick's practical help.
- The party still learns the machine can be saved and that Tidewick is nearby.
- Segment 2 occurs through observation rather than a successful check; no clue route closes.

### Act 1 escalation triggers

- A short rest: advance one segment after showing a gentler omen first.
- Forceful repeated probing of the Hearthstone: do not advance automatically; on the second such attempt, advance if the party has been warned that it aggravates the connection.
- Leaving Gannet in the surf beyond the ninety-minute window: a nonessential system corrodes; walking repair takes longer, but the machine is not destroyed.
- Sharing the full story loudly near the dune path: begin rumor propagation one step early.

## Act 2: Tidewick Reacts

Act 2 may occur in Tidewick, at the beach with locals arriving, or through a split party. These events still move.

### Automatic events

1. **Sela mobilizes:** Boatwright Sela Rook loads rollers, rope, and block-and-tackle after Mara's report.
2. **Edrin compares signs:** Brother Edrin Vale finds that shrine cords have tightened into unfamiliar patterns. He can identify infernal-contract residue but cannot name a culprit or purpose.
3. **Jory arrives:** Courier Jory Fen reaches Tidewick with inland road reports. Jory has no direct report from Olive or Brackenmill unless a later act establishes one.
4. **The story fragments:** By the middle of the act, locals refer to “the iron wreck,” “the cave people,” and “the humming stone.”

### Act 2 escalation triggers

- Public magical display around the Hearthstone: add one sensational rumor.
- Contradictory party accounts: Nims records both; trust does not collapse, but later questions become sharper.
- Asking officials to quarantine the beach: movement slows and the courier hears an official version.
- Repairing Gannet with Sela's full help: time passes one segment, but travel capability improves substantially.
- Taking a short rest: advance normally and allow one useful private NPC conversation.

### Act 2 consequences, not punishments

- If the party is open, practical help arrives sooner and rumors are more accurate.
- If the party is secretive, privacy holds temporarily, but locals fill gaps with mundane theories before supernatural ones.
- If the party bypasses town, Sela still goes to inspect the machine and Jory still carries a distorted beach story inland.
- If the party sends a message, it leaves on schedule even if no reply can arrive this session.

## Transition and Act 3: Return to Brackenmill

Act 3 occurs at Pell's hut in distant Brackenmill. Resolve the journey with the campaign's established travel method. If none exists, use **Gannet's Hearthline Return**: once stabilized, Gannet can lock onto its workshop's home-bearing through the Resonant Hearthstone and cross the distance in a hard, uncanny travel montage. Tidewick's ceramic collar makes the passage smooth; without it, Pell spends several additional hours improvising a safe collar and sacrifices a nonessential machine system. Either way, travel is possible and no Tidewick activity is mandatory.

### Automatic events

1. **Tidewick continues offscreen:** Jory leaves with the entrusted account, Sela recovers her tackle, and Edrin records the omen. These facts matter next session but do not interrupt the rescue.
2. **Hearthline bearing:** The stone transmits the strongest Brackenmill impression yet—Pell's hut, oil, woodsmoke, restraint, and living distress—allowing Gannet to find home.
3. **Olive answers:** At the hut, Olive deliberately repeats a simple tap after Pell. This is the first certain communication and establishes her agency.
4. **The Engine wakes:** Gannet docks into the hut's flywheel and the Concordance Engine begins calibration.

### Act 3 escalation triggers

- Taking extra hours to perfect Gannet: Olive begins Act 3 at Trust 1 rather than 2, but the machine gains one Spare token.
- Forcing the Hearthstone during transit after a warning: begin Act 3 with +1 Strain and an Insight token.
- Honoring Olive's first stop signal: remove 1 starting Strain, if any, or gain 1 Trust.
- Skipping calibration to start immediately: begin round 1 with +1 Strain; Pell states this cost before activation.
- Public accusations made in Tidewick still enter Jory's account as claims, never established truth.

### End-of-session state

At the end of Act 3, record:

- Olive's outcome tier, condition, wishes, and first chosen words or signals;
- party location and intended next step;
- Gannet Nine, the Engine, and Hearthstone's final condition;
- what message, if any, Jory carries;
- who knows Olive's name and actual location;
- current clock segment;
- strongest confirmed resonance clue;
- leading rumors and who believes them;
- any explicit player theory about motive, clearly labeled **unconfirmed**.

## Tidewick NPC Movement

These are background paths, not rails. Move an NPC early if the party seeks them.

### Mara Venn — fisherwoman

**Voice:** Low, blunt, patient coastal cadence.  
**Wants:** Protect the spawning beach, get practical help to people in trouble, and keep Tidewick accurately informed.  
**Tell:** Tests net knots while judging sincerity.  
**Secret:** Her net formed an eye-like loop at dawn; she cut it away.

| Clock | Location | Activity |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Dune crest | Watches the machine and party in plain view. |
| 2 | Beach | Offers Tidewick's resources and shares the omen if trust is earned. |
| 3 | Dune path/boatyard | Carries an accurate first report to Sela. |
| 4 | Harbor edge | Corrects claims that Olive is in Tidewick. |
| 5 | Beach or inn | Helps with tackle or challenges embellished rumors. |
| 6 | Home quay | Repairs the cut net and watches its knots. |

### Sela Rook — master boatwright

**Voice:** Clipped, resonant, speaks in measurements and material limits.  
**Wants:** Get Gannet above the tide, prevent damage to her tackle, and learn how its joints bear weight.  
**Tell:** Measures spans with thumb and little finger when thinking.  
**Secret:** She keeps a salvaged ceramic bearing collar from an old tide engine that may fit Gannet; she will offer it if Pell treats her as a collaborator, not hired labor.

| Clock | Location | Activity |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Boatyard | Supervises a keel repair; unaware of the party. |
| 2 | Boatyard | Hears Mara's report and selects gear. |

**Voice:** Gentle, formal, pauses before naming supernatural conclusions.  
**Wants:** Distinguish omen from panic, relieve suffering, and make an accurate record.  
**Tell:** Reties his sleeve cord whenever a claim lacks evidence.  
**Secret:** Years ago he witnessed a contract curse whose victim mistook compulsion for desire. He fears interpreting Olive's condition as consent, guilt, or intent.

## Olive's Worsening Condition

Use these descriptions sparingly and only through legitimate resonance, divination, a direct scene cut, or later evidence. Olive has agency even while restrained; do not reduce her to a passive countdown.

| Clock | Physical condition | Possible act of agency | What the party can perceive |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Eyes and mouth sewn shut; breathing controlled but frightened. | Tests whether the twine responds to movement. | Taut thread, darkness, muffled breath. |
| 2 | Twine tightens; skin is chafed and cut. | Stops struggling physically and listens for patterns. | A pulse that changes after her name. |
| 3 | Fatigue and thirst increase. | Attempts a deliberate two-beat response. | The Hearthstone's double pulse. |
| 4 | Breathing is shallower; infernal residue grows clearer. | Uses touch, footfalls, or memory to orient herself within the hut. | Floorboards, oil, woodsmoke, constriction. |
| 5 | Exhaustion deepens; restraint remains active. | Tries to anchor a memory or name through the resonance. | A fleeting sense of Pell's hut and living presence. |
| 6 | Grave distress but not a predetermined death. | Conserves strength or attempts one final signal; choose later based on play. | One forceful pulse, silence, then a faint return. |

### Boundaries on interpretation

- Twine tightening after resistance shows a control mechanism, not the controller's emotional response.
- A pulse after Olive's name suggests connection, not proof that she heard spoken words.
- Infernal residue shows contract magic, not who signed, who benefits, or why.
- Olive's attempted signals may be conscious or reflexive until the fiction earns certainty.
- Do not speak dialogue through Olive's sewn mouth. Mental communication, if rules permit it, may convey impressions or thoughts.

## Signs and Omens

Choose signs that reinforce the current scene; do not stack enough to imply an imminent attack.

### Subtle

- A loose thread aligns inland.
- Three nearby knots tighten by the same fraction.
- A cup's rim hums at the pulse interval.
- Shadows of dune grass briefly resemble crossed strands.
- A compass hesitates between north and Brackenmill's bearing.
- Written loops in ink seem too tight for a blink, then appear normal.

### Moderate

- A net forms an eye-like oval.
- A bell rope knots itself once while hanging still.
- The Hearthstone pulses twice when Olive's name is spoken.
- Reflections show faint strands across the viewer's eyes or lips.
- Pell's diagnostic lens fogs except for one inland line.

### Strong

- Every cord in one small room draws taut at once.
- A small flame goes out on a single heavy pulse.
- The sound of thread pulled through cloth crosses the resonance.
- The stone gives a brief, unmistakable sensory impression of Pell's hut.

Strong signs advance clarity, not combat.

## Rumor Propagation

Track rumors by source, accuracy, and reach. Rumors never override established facts.

### Starting rumors

1. **“An iron giant washed ashore.”**  
   Source: beach watcher. Mostly false; Gannet is Pell's machine and did not wash ashore.

2. **“The cave survivors brought out a singing stone.”**  
   Source: Mara's report filtered through the harbor. Broadly true, sensational wording.

3. **“Someone in Tidewick has been thread-cursed.”**  
   Source: combined knot omens. False location; Olive is in distant Brackenmill.

4. **“The old shore shrine predicted this.”**  
   Source: regulars at the inn. False; Edrin is investigating after the fact.

5. **“The machine can follow the curse.”**  
   Source: overheard repair talk. Partly true; its compass can hold the bearing.

### Propagation steps

| Step | Reach | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Whisper | 1–3 NPCs | Easy to correct privately. |
| Harbor talk | Workers and inn patrons | Creates questions and offers of help. |
| Town version | Most active residents | Nims condenses one account as “what happened.” |
| Courier version | Leaves Tidewick | Persists and may arrive inland before or after the party. |

### Changing a rumor

- A firsthand factual account reduces one false element automatically.
- Physical evidence corrects a location or mechanism but not unknown motive.
- A successful **DC 12 Persuasion** makes the corrected version the one Nims records.
- On a failed check, Nims records both versions and labels their sources; the truth is not erased.
- Threats or obvious deception make the story more dramatic, not necessarily more hostile.

## Escalation Menu

Use an escalation when triggered or when a long scene needs a visible consequence. Never use more than one major escalation in the same scene.

### Social

- Two residents arrive with incompatible but nonviolent offers.
- A rumor names Tidewick as Olive's location; Mara publicly corrects it.
- Nims asks the party for a quotable account before Jory leaves.
- Sela demands a clear decision because her crew and tackle cannot wait through the tide.

### Practical

- Tide reaches an unsecured machine panel.
- The courier begins sealing the final packet.
- Daylight wanes, altering travel comfort but not making travel impossible.
- A needed workshop space becomes available for one hour.

### Resonant

- Olive's name evokes a double pulse.
- Contract residue becomes detectable by a prepared ritual.
- A sign appears farther from the Hearthstone than before.
- The strongest sensory echo yet points to Pell's hut.

### Emotional

- Pell admits he ignored Olive's fear of loose thread.
- Mara reveals she cut away the eye-shaped net.
- Edrin warns that compulsion must not be mistaken for intent.
- A PC briefly experiences harmless sympathetic silence or darkness.
