# Act Three — Free Olive
### 45–60 minutes

## Premise

Olive’s eyes and mouth are bound by tuned puppet twine.

The **resonant heartstone** breaks it with **frequency**:

1. Find the twine’s note  
2. Build a counter-frequency  
3. Push until the strings break  

Act Two prep (black iron, music box, lighthouse, frame) makes this safer. It is **not** required to begin.

Need: heartstone + Olive’s consent.

---

# Setup

## Checklist

- [ ] Heartstone carrier known  
- [ ] Olive agrees  
- [ ] Location picked  
- [ ] Every PC has a job  

## Locations

| Place | Why |
|---|---|
| Tinkerer’s workshop | Default — tools, quiet, mech brace |
| Lighthouse | Strongest natural resonance |
| Olive’s cabin | Calmest emotionally |
| Church | Powerful residue, louder risk |

## Jobs

1. Stone-bearer  
2. Listener (higher / lower / lock)  
3. Stabilizer (Olive’s breath / calm)  
4. Catcher (snapping thread / frame)  
5. Guard / amplifier (music box, voice, bell, weapon ring)

## Read aloud

> Olive sits where you placed her. The black twine goes taut, as if it knows what you brought.
>
> The resonant heartstone warms. One low note fills the room — not loud, but everywhere, like a tuning fork inside bone.
>
> The Tinkerer: “We are not cutting her face. We find the frequency that owns those strings… then we play the note that tells them they are finished.”

## Ask Olive

1 tap = yes · 2 = slowly · closed hand = stop  

If stop → stop. Try later.

# Frequency Break

## Phase 1 — Find the twine’s note

Players listen with the heartstone. Skills: Arcana, Investigation, Perception, Insight, Performance, Medicine, tools.

**Tell:**

> The black thread vibrates on its own — a thin wrong note, half a step off anything human. The heartstone answers, hunting it.

**2–3 checks, DC 12–14** (different PCs)

| Successes | Result |
|---|---|
| 2+ | Lock clean |
| 1 | Found, slips — one Phase 2 roll at disadvantage |
| 0 | Stone shrieks once (Panic +1 if public); Tinkerer finds a rough match; Phase 3 harder |

Always continue.

## Phase 2 — Build the counter-frequency

> “Same note breaks nothing. You need the cancel.”

Ideas: aim through black iron · music-box lullaby against the wrong note · tide-bell memory · two tones opposite ways · Olive squeezes when it feels right.

**2–3 checks, DC 13**  
Advantage if: black iron / crank / lighthouse practice / Olive’s trust.

| Result | Effect |
|---|---|
| Strong | Notes grind; strings thin |
| Partial | Unstable — one PC must “hold pitch” in Phase 3 |
| Fail | Spike; painful not lethal; retry once; second fail → Phase 3 at partial ceiling only |

## Phase 3 — Break the twine

> Pressure behind the eyes. Twine lifts a hair’s width. The heartstone climbs toward a third note.

**3 beats**, one PC each: push stone · keep Olive synced · catch failing thread.  
Each beat: DC 14 (or strong description).

| Total successes | Outcome |
|---|---|
| 3 | **Full break** — eyes and mouth free |
| 2 | **Partial** — eyes OR mouth free |
| 1 | **Stabilized** — loose; finish next session |
| 0 | **Abort** — stone cold; Olive unhurt; method known |

**Teddy (optional, budget left):** private — his wrist sings the same wrong note for one second.

**Collector:** one flavor line max (“Intermission”) or distant applause. No courtroom.

# Outcomes

### Full

> Twine goes white-hot with silent vibration, then powders into ash that never quite lands. She breathes. The heartstone falls quiet.

She is free. Don’t secretly leave her controlled.

### Eyes only

> “Don’t look up. He’s listening through what’s left.”

### Mouth only

> “Not… his.”

### Stabilized

> “We found the frequency. We didn’t finish the song. That’s enough for tonight.”

Still a win.

2. One town reaction.  
3. Update `clocks.md`.  
4. **One** cliffhanger, then stop.

### Cliffhangers (pick one)

- Applause + card: *Act Two begins when the understudy forgets his lines. — P.*  
- Heartstone’s last note — not Olive’s. Closer than the cave.

Say: **“That is where we stop tonight.”** No explaining after the cut.
