# DM Crib — NPCs & What Ifs

## Voices (want / fear / line)

**Tinkerer** — Free Olive safely / His past mistakes made this worse  
> “Tonight’s job is frequencies, not paperwork.”

**Olive** — Be asked, not managed / Treated as absent  
Silent: taps, drawings. Clever, stubborn — not a pity prop.

**Caretaker / Purdy** — Olive safe / Spectacle  
> “She likes being asked.”

**Bram** — Finish weapons / Black-iron sale helped the killer  
> “That is not an excuse. It is the shape of my mistake.”

**Halloway (Watch)** — Real evidence / Wrongful hanging of Neri  
> “Useful is better than frightening.”

**Neri** — Believed / Water  
> “I cut the strings. They were inside him.”

**Ambrose (puppets)** — Not blamed for murders / Town destroys his craft  
> “Some things take you politely.”

**Lyra** — Believed about the lights / Called a fool  
> “Things that hum out here, you throw back.”

**Ashur / Mr. Strings** — Collect performances / Free choice he can’t file  
Does **not** appear bodily. One line max at climax: “Intermission.”

## What if…

**Ignore Olive** — Let them. Tick Olive after 2 unrelated stops. At 6, crisis enters their scene.

**Cut strings immediately** — She signals stop. Cutting without cancel: thread retreats under skin; stone shrieks; no permanent harm. Teaches: need counter-frequency.

**Split party** — Who carries the heartstone? 5-min cuts.

**Throw / smash / sell stone** — Returns at low tide · splits into two weaker halves · recoverable by favor/Watch/theft.

**Leave town** — Allowed. Notice appears on the boat. Tidewick keeps changing.

**Accuse Ambrose** — Not secretly evil because they suspect him. “Don’t confuse resemblance with guilt.”

**Attack Watch** — Restraint, not slaughter. Panic +1. Evidence still reachable.

**Hell poster deal** — “State consideration precisely.” Vague = void. Small = thin mark. Heavy = thread + Ledger +1. Rescind with Tinkerer before next 11th bell.

**Expose Teddy** — Check his player. Fear, not a mob. Olive: connection ≠ guilt.

**Fight Mr. Strings** — Reflection/puppet: “Not yet.” Goes limp.

**Stuck** — Restate what they know → exactly 3 choices → least-used NPC interrupts → free clue → “What matters most to your character?”
