# Minigames

Use **2–3** in Act Two. Act Three’s climax is **Frequency Break** (full rules in `03-act-three.md`).

Failure never soft-locks the session.

---

## Frequency Break (Act Three)

1. Find twine note — DC 12–14 × 2–3  
2. Build cancel — DC 13 × 2–3 (adv: iron / crank / lighthouse / trust)  
3. Break — DC 14 × 3 beats → 3 full / 2 partial / 1 stabilize / 0 abort  

Ask Olive first.

---

## Marionette’s Heart (Tinkerer)

Fix resonance frame — 3× DC 12 (gear / wire / damper), different PCs.  
3 clean · 2 babysit · 0–1 sparks once. Never blocks Act Three.

---

## Temper & Strike (Bram)

Heat → Fold → Quench. Each: describe + true sentence + DC 13.  
3 excellent+property · 2 excellent · 0–1 functional + favor.

---

## Ship, Captain, Crew (Tavern/docks)

5d6, up to 3 rolls. Set aside in order: 6 Ship → 5 Captain → 4 Crew. Other two = Cargo. Highest Cargo wins a true rumor.

---

## Bones & Bells (Tavern)

Secret 3d6. Bid totals or call “Bells!” Three quick rounds. Winner: true rumor. Others: garbled.

---

## Quiet Game (Olive)

Max 3 questions. 1=yes · 2=no · tap=wait · palm=complicated. No check if respectful.

---

## Resonance Tuning (Lighthouse/church prep)

Silence → match note → lock stone. 3× DC 13.  
3 = advantage on one Act Three phase · 2 = useful frequency · 0–1 = brief hint then quiet.

---

## Three Truths of the Cave (Tavern)

Each PC: 2 true cave details + 1 lie. Table guesses. Winner: Inspiration.
