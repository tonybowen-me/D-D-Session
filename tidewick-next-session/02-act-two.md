# Act Two — Town
### 2–2.5 hours · run 3–4 locations, not all

**Loop:** arrive → clue → NPC/minigame → 2–3 exits → check `clocks.md`

**Go to Act Three when:** they have the heartstone + Olive’s consent and want to try, or ~2.5–3 hours in.

If they split: ask who carries the heartstone; 5-minute cuts.

---

# A. Olive
### 20–25 min

## Read aloud

> The cabin smells of wool, tea, and rain.
>
> Olive sits beneath the window, shoes pointed together. Black twine runs from her closed eyes into her hair and from her lips to her collar.
>
> The thread is not taut. That is worse — it rests there like a hand on a shoulder.

If Purdy / caretaker is present:

> “Quiet, please. She hears floors better than voices now.”

## Jobs for idle PCs

- Door / window (no forced entry; window opened from inside)
- Watch across the street (someone is watching; curtain drops)
- Music box (crank missing; herring scales + blue ribbon on spindle)
- Ask caretaker questions

## Three Olive beats

1. **Permission** — ask before approaching; she offers a white ribbon. “She likes being asked.”
2. **Drawing** — tall figure, gold cuffs, strings, herself in a circle, second unfinished circle (don’t force Teddy).
3. **Music box** — empty crank; points harbor → herself → heartstone.

## Quiet Game (max 3 questions)

1 squeeze = yes · 2 = no · tap = wait · open palm = complicated

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Did the church attacker bind you? | Yes, then tap: someone else commanded it |
| Is the Tinkerer trying to hurt you? | No |
| Will the stone help? | Yes; points to her heart first |
| Are your parents alive? | Open palm; unknown |
| Is the music important? | Yes |

At ~15 min: “She’s tiring.” End.

**Exits:** Tinkerer (frequency plan) · Harbor (crank) · Church (string pitch)

# B. Tinkerer’s workshop
### 25–30 min

## Read aloud

> Tools, frequency charts, and mech parts fill the room. Olive’s painted handprints mark a control yoke under soot.

## Mech fun first (safe, funny)

Let one PC: stack barrels, lift a boat, arm-wrestle, wave without wrecking a shelf.

## The plan (this is Act Three)

> “The twine isn’t just rope. It’s tuned. The heartstone can hear that note. Match it, then push a cancel — a counter-frequency — and the strings lose their grip and break.
>
> We are not cutting her face. We are breaking the song that owns her.”

**Need:** heartstone + Olive’s consent + quiet place.  
**Optional (helps):** black iron · music box · prior lighthouse/church listen · resonance frame.

## Minigame: Marionette’s Heart

Fix the resonance frame (3 parts, DC 12 each — different PCs):

1. Gear (Sleight/tools)  
2. Wire path (Investigation/Arcana)  
3. Damper (Perception/Athletics)

3 = clean · 2 = needs babysitting in Act Three · 0–1 = works once, sparks. Never blocks Act Three.

## Bram ask

> “Bring me a shaving of black iron. Not the whole ingot.”

**Exits:** Bram · Olive · Lighthouse/church · Act Three if ready

# C. Bram’s forge
### 30–40 min

## Read aloud

> Weapons wait under canvas, tagged with names and hand sketches.
>
> “You promised you’d come back. I kept the fire lit. Don’t make me melt these into nails.”

Ask what each weapon is *for* → tiny story property (protection / precision vs thread / vengeance vs Contracts / mercy).

## Minigame: Temper & Strike

3 rounds (Heat → Fold → Quench). Each: describe + one true sentence to the steel + DC 13 check.

3 = excellent + property · 2 = excellent · 0–1 = functional + Bram owes a favor.

## Black iron

> “Bad night pretending to be metal. Tall customer. Gold thread at the cuffs. Child-sized carving knives. Coin that rang underwater.”

**Teddy (only if <2 symptoms):** stone + iron = two notes; private wrist vibration. Mark Teddy ☐.

**Rewards:** weapons · black-iron shaving · black coin · Watch testimony · help in Act Three if invited

**Exits:** Tinkerer · Tavern · Watch

# E. Optional — Investigation (pick 1–2)
### 20–40 min total

### Church
Contract No. 1 on the floor. Puppet-string bell rope. Stone + rope = shriek, Panic +1. Residual “wrong pitch” helps Act Three.

### Watch
Evidence trade. Map is a spiral centered on Olive’s street. Prisoner **Neri**: cut strings that were *inside* a body; heard a bell under water. Don’t spend the whole night arguing his release.

### Mortuary
Joint wounds, little blood, deaths 11–midnight, Contract No. 4 on unclaimed body. Ledger +1 per tag found.
