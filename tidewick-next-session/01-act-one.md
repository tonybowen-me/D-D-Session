# Act One — Arrival
### 20–30 minutes

## Goals

- Party out of the cave with the resonant heartstone
- Tinkerer present (injured, functional)
- Teddy gets **one** private symptom
- Hint: long-fingered stranger asked about Olive
- Hand them destinations and ask where first

Move inland as soon as they commit. Don’t milk the beach.

---

# 1. Beach

## Read aloud

> Each withdrawing wave steals sand from beneath your feet, as though the shore has decided your footprints are an argument.
>
> Offshore, the Tinkerer floats in the cockpit of his mech, working gears and levers, trying to make it functional again.
>
> Between you lies the resonant heartstone. It gives one low note — not quite sound. The feeling of glass remembering it was once sand.
>
> Over the intercom: “Nobody move it.”

Pause.

Then:

> “No. Move it if it burns you. Put it down if it sings. Throw it into the sea only if it says a name you love.”

## Teddy only

> Your wrist flicks. Your fingers twist — moving on their own, just slightly.

Mark **TEDDY ☐** in `clocks.md`.

## Tinkerer answers

**Hurt?**  
> “Bruised ribs. Pride punctured through. The mech took the cliff personally.”

**Olive?**  
> “She’s still back at my cabin. My robotic children are watching her.”

**Stone?**  
> “It hears what places are trying not to remember. Don’t ask it questions. Give it something honest and see what answers.”

## One beach clue (pick one)

| Clue | Points to |
|---|---|
| Wooden puppet finger, initials **A.W.** | Puppet shop (Ambrose) |
| Bottle: “…cut them before the tide turns…” | Olive / lighthouse |
| Black coin that rings underwater against the stone | Bram’s black iron |

## Examining the stone

Free facts: pitch changes with attention; quieter when wrapped; responds to connections; doesn’t answer questions.

Checks (optional): DC 12 = causes other things to resonate; DC 15 = recognizes matching wounds / materials. Failure still gets DC 12 fact.

## Leave the beach

Ask:

> “Who is carrying the resonant heartstone?”

Write it in `clocks.md`.

> “Town is that way: smoke, bells, forge.”

---

# 2. Lyra (optional, short)

> A fisherwoman stops at the muffled hum. “Things that hum out here, you throw back.”

If they talk:

- Three lanterns on open water, no boat
- Peddler with fingers too long, asking about the girl with sewn eyes
- *Merry Widow* vanished; her bell came back alone

They can ignore her.

---

# 3. Reveal town

> Tidewick descends toward the sea. Forge smoke. Church steeple. Docks. One cottage with every curtain closed. A puppet shop window full of wooden faces.

**Simple destination list (player-facing):**

1. Olive / Tinkerer’s cabin  
2. Bram’s forge  
3. Church / Watch (murders)  
4. Tavern (Drowned Bell)  
5. Harbor / lighthouse  
6. Puppet shop  

Ask:

> “Tidewick is open. Where first?”

If they stall, offer only three: Olive, Bram, or investigation.
