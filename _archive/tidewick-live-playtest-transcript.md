## ROUND 1 — BEACH OPENING

**DM:**  
Cold surf drags at your limbs as you cough up seawater and blink into grey morning light. The beach is littered with driftwood and shattered crate-slats from the wreck, and half-buried in the wet sand beside you, a smooth grey stone hums — a low, tuneful vibration you feel in your teeth more than hear. A few yards off, **Cobb** groans and rolls onto his side, spitting sand, one hand already groping for a weapon that isn't there. Nearby, where the foam draws back, brass fingers glint and twitch in the tide — **Longarm's** mechanical hand, alone, its wrist-joint clicking softly with each retreating wave, as if still trying to grab hold of something that isn't there anymore.

*[TO TEDDY ONLY]: As the brass fingers click in the surf, you feel an answering twitch — a phantom clench in your own wrist, in time with it. Just for a heartbeat. Then it's gone.*

The resonance stone's hum rises very slightly, like it's noticed you're awake.

What do you do?

**Mira:** I push up onto my knees, still coughing, and scramble toward Cobb. "Easy—I'm here. Can you breathe?" My eyes flick to the humming stone, then to that brass hand twitching in the foam. "Stay still a moment. Something's wrong, and I want to know you're whole before we touch anything."

**Teddy:** "Easy—easy. Nobody panic. I'm fine." He pushes up on one knee, eyes flicking to Longarm's brass hand, then rubs his own wrist like he's shaking off the cold. A beat later he edges toward the humming stone, voice low. "That thing singing to itself, or am I still half-drowned?"

**Kael:** I wade in and grab Longarm's brass hand before the tide takes it. "Cobb—up. We'll find your steel after." I plant myself between the others and the humming stone, eyes on the dunes. "Nobody touch that rock yet."

**Sylv:** "Oh—listen. That hum just changed key the moment we arrived." Sylv crouches by the wet sand, fingertips hovering over the grey resonance stone without quite touching it yet, eyes bright. "Cobb, stay with us—Longarm, your hand's keeping time with the tide. Stone? What story are you trying to sing?"

**Nyx:** teeth buzz—stone first. I crouch, palm hover over the hum, not touching. "Cobb—frequency spike on wake. Intentional, or ambient?" Eye the brass hand once. "Longarm can wait. Data won't."

## ROUND 2 — COBB WARNS / STONE LISTENS

**DM:**  
Mira's hands steady Cobb as he coughs, then manages a thin, wry smile. "Breathing. Mostly. Don't touch that rock—sings wrong. Olive's inland, Tidewick. She'll—" He winces, pressing a palm to his ribs. "Just don't let it near your ears."

Kael's grip closes on cold brass; the fingers twitch once against his palm, then still. The stone's hum brightens a half-step as Teddy, Sylv, and Nyx lean in—attention thick as fog—then settles into a lower, waiting drone, as if listening back.

Beyond the dunes, a thin chimney-line of smoke marks town.

What do you do?

**Mira:** I keep a hand at Cobb's shoulder and glance toward the dunes—smoke means people, and Olive. "He's right. Don't lean in, any of you—not Teddy, not Sylv. It can wait." I help Cobb sit up a little, careful of his ribs. "Town's close. We find Olive first, then we argue with singing rocks."

**Teddy:** "Heard the man—rock stays put." He takes a half-step back from the stone, flexing his fingers once like they're just numb from the surf. "Smoke's inland. Tidewick. We move before that drone decides it wants an audience."

**Kael:** I tuck the brass hand into my belt and haul Cobb toward solid ground. "Stone stays. We don't. Smoke's Tidewick—Bram's waiting, and Olive's already ahead." I point inland. "On your feet. We're moving."

**Sylv:** "Wrong song it is, then—I won't feed it my ears." Sylv rises, dusting wet sand from their palms, gaze flicking from the waiting drone of the stone to the smoke inland. "Olive's in Tidewick, and that smoke looks like a story already halfway told. We move—before this rock decides to finish the verse without us."

**Nyx:** "Noted—no contact, no ears." Circles the stone at arm's length, tracing the drone's pitch with two fingers in air. "Cobb: waiting tone after attention spike. Dormant, or listening? I need a safe sample method before Tidewick."

## ROUND 3 — LYRA + TOWN OPENS

**DM:**  
Cobb catches Kael's belt and the wrapped bundle. "Cloth. Tight. Not near ears." The stone's drone muffles as someone knots a wet shirt around it; then you're moving—brass hand at Kael's hip, Cobb between Mira and solid ground.

Halfway up the dune path, a fisherwoman in salt-stiff oilskins blocks the way just long enough to stare at the wrapped hum. **Lyra Quill** spit-touches her thumb. "Things that hum out here, you throw back. Last night: lantern-lights over the water. No boat. And a peddler with fingers too long, asking after the girl with sewn eyes." She steps aside without waiting for thanks.

You crest the rise. **Tidewick** opens below—forge smoke from Bram's, church steeple, docks, Loom Street cottage curtains (Olive), a tavern sign swinging, a puppet shop perched on the hill.

Tidewick is open. Where first?

**Mira:** "Loom Street. Now." I start that way without waiting for a vote. "Someone was asking after Olive—fingers too long—and she's been alone through all of this. Forge and docks can wait; I need to see her first."

**Teddy:** "Olive first—Loom Street. Cobb said she knows, and a girl with sewn eyes ain't a tavern rumor you chase after dark." He glances uphill toward the puppet shop and keeps his hands loose at his sides. "Hill shop can wait. Places that make dolls always want more string."

**Kael:** "Olive first—she's alone and that peddler talk doesn't sit right. Quick look, then Bram's forge. I want real steel before we chase sewn-eye stories." I start toward Loom Street. "If Mira's already headed there, even better—we don't split for long."

**Sylv:** "Olive on Loom Street, yes—but that peddler just handed us the opening line: a girl with sewn eyes, and a puppet shop on the hill." Sylv points upslope, already half-turned, voice bright with hunger. "Stories like that don't wait politely. Puppet shop first—if Olive's tangled in this town's wrong song, that's where the verse starts."

**Nyx:** "Olive can wait one hour. Wrapped doesn't mean inert." Gestures toward the church—quiet, stone walls, fewer ears. "I need baseline readings before we drag a singing rock into her cottage. Who's coming, who's sentimental?"

## ROUND 4 — THE SPLIT

**DM:**  
The path forks under salt-wind and chimney smoke. Mira, Teddy, Kael, and Cobb take Loom Street. Sylv points upslope—Nyx follows, muttering about lost baselines.

**[GROUP A — Loom Street]**  
Mrs. Purdy opens before you knock, wool-smell and tea spilling out. "Oh—Cobb. Come in. Quiet, please." Inside, **Olive** sits under the window, black thread resting neat across her eyes and mouth—not knotted, just *held*. On the shelf: a music box, crank gone. The cottage listens.

**[GROUP B — Whistlecraft]**  
The marionette shop door sighs open. Every doll faces you. If you look close: six fingers on more than one hand. From the back room, soft footsteps—**Ambrose Whistlecraft**, smiling too early. "Customers. Or pilgrims. Which?"

What do you do? (say which group you're in)

### Group A

**Mira:** I ease in behind Cobb and murmur, "Thank you, Mrs. Purdy." Then I kneel by Olive, voice soft. "I'm here. You're safe." I don't touch the thread yet—just watch her breathing. "May I look? Only if it doesn't hurt."

**Teddy:** He slips in on soft soles, voice barely a breath. "Thread's deliberate—don't yank it." Eyes flick to the music box's empty crank-slot, then back to Olive. "Crank's gone. Someone wanted the song stopped."

**Kael:** I leave Mira the talking and check the door latch, then the window shutters—nothing forced from outside. I plant myself at the threshold, eyes on Loom Street. "Thread's not coming near her ears on my watch. Call if you need hands."

### Group B

**Sylv:** "Pilgrim, if the pilgrimage is a good rumor—and yours have six fingers if you count carefully." Sylv tips a small bow toward the watching dolls, smile bright, not quite stepping closer. "Sylv. I collect verses the way you collect faces. Ambrose Whistlecraft—tell me which of these is listening hardest."

**Nyx:** counts six fingers, ignores the choir of painted stares. "Neither. Researchers who lost a quiet church for... this." Taps the wrapped stone once through cloth. "Your dolls track us. Design feature, or something listening? And Ambrose—why the extra digit?"

## OBSERVATIONS FROM THIS LIVE RUN

1. **Five distinct voices actually argued** — Mira/Teddy/Kael → Olive; Sylv → puppet shop; Nyx → church. The fork was player-driven, not DM-scripted.
2. **Kael self-assigned the door job** at Olive's without being told — validates the "idle fighter needs a physical job" patch (he invented it organically).
3. **Teddy hid the wrist tell** as "cold" / "numb" — breadcrumb #1 landed without table explosion.
4. **Nyx brought the wrapped stone into Whistlecraft** and marionettes tilted — stone-as-toy worked; risk: Group B now has the MacGuffin away from Olive (Group A has Olive without the stone's full presence — stone was with Nyx). **Design note:** Clarify who carries the stone when the party splits; this nearly separated cure tool from cure target.
5. **Sylv's six-finger line before Ambrose explained it** — player skill noticing; good.
6. **Strong start → Lyra → open town** flowed in 3 DM beats without paralysis until the destination vote — then healthy conflict, then split.

### Patch from THIS playtest
**STONE CARRIER RULE:** When the party splits, ask aloud: "Who is carrying the resonance stone?" Default: it stays with the Olive-bound group unless Nyx-type PCs explicitly take it. If split with stone elsewhere, Olive scene still works; ritual later requires reunion.
