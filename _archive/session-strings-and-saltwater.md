# Session Bible: Strings & Salt Water
### Exploratory Town Session (No Forced Combat)

**Tone:** Dark fairy tale — creepy-beautiful, not gore-porn  
**Length:** 3–4+ hours if they roam  
**Design philosophy:** Prep situations, not a plot. Nodes + leads. Soft clocks. Curiosity rewarded.

### What is NOT in the shell
- No “you must free Olive tonight”
- No required boss fight
- No single correct path
- No chokepoint NPC who holds the only clue

### Soft clocks (run quietly; advance on scene transitions)

| Clock | Segments | Advances when… | At full… |
|-------|----------|----------------|----------|
| **Olive’s stillness** | 6 | Party ignores Olive 2+ nodes in a row; uncle attempts alone | Partial botched cut attempt (hurt, not dead) — next-session hook |
| **Town panic** | 8 | Public scares, stone shrieks, funerals, ignored Watch | Gates locked early; social friction ↑; ritual harder (noise) |
| **Hell’s ledger** | 6 | They find Contract tags / speak BBEG’s title aloud / applause | A polite notice appears somewhere they just were |

Clocks create pressure without stealing choice. Partial fills are texture; full fills are complications, not TPKs.

### Stakes questions (genuinely undecided — play to find out)
1. Will Olive be fully freed, partially freed, or left for next session?
2. Does the town trust the party as saviors, spectacles, or liabilities?
3. Does anyone notice Teddy’s string?
4. Is the Puppet Master collecting a *soul*, a *performance*, or a *debt* — and whose?

# PART C — SETTING KIT

## Town name: **Tidewick**
Salt-worn fishing town on a tidal estuary. Recently: overnight murders; church fight; cave earthquake felt as “the mountain screamed.” Tonight the town is exhausted, superstitious, and half-shut.

### Districts (pointcrawl labels for your map handout)
1. **Knotted Quay** — docks  
2. **Gullbone Common** — square / market  
3. **Tallow Row** — smithy, workshops  
4. **Low Mercy** — church + mortuary  
5. **Loom Street** — Olive’s cottage, residential  
6. **Bellglass Ward** — lighthouse approach / pier  
7. **Nine Shutters** — tavern alleys  
8. **Red-Skein Hill** — puppet shop, odd crafts  

### Recurring motifs (environmental storytelling)
- **Six fingers** on nearly every marionette  
- **Brass Contract tags** (`CONTRACT No. X`)  
- **Lullaby** that appears in three places  
- **Black iron** that hums “wrong”  
- **Doll-bread** sold “for luck” after the murders  
- **Applause** from nowhere at emotional peaks  

### BBEG framing (drip, don’t dump)
**Ashur Vail** — street-name **Mr. Strings** — Hell’s #1 bounty hunter.  
Title options: *Writ-Catcher*, *Red Auditor*, *First of the Stringed Ledger*.  
He doesn’t just kill — he makes victims **finish their own last act**. Ranking is by *style of collection*. Olive is unfinished performance / key witness. Teddy may be an **understudy**.

Infernal flavor (from contract-devil lore): Hell is legalistic. He leaves **receipts**. Destroying or annotating paperwork matters. He cannot casually break his own clauses — leverage for later arcs.

# PART D — PHASE 0: BEACH LANDING (10–15 min)

Party teleports/drops onto wet sand with the **resonance stone**. Uncle’s **Longarm** gundam smokes half-buried if you want him present; or he staggered ahead and left a trail of scorched footprints.

### Sensory menu (pick 4–5)
- Tide foam that briefly looks like white thread, then dissolves  
- Gulls refuse to land near the stone  
- One object is impossibly dry (stone, a PC’s pocket, Olive’s shoe)  
- Crab investigates the stone, taps it, flees when it hums  
- Footprints leading *away* from them that aren’t theirs  
- Far inland: town bells, slightly off-tempo  

### Beach discoveries (optional, 1–2)
1. **Wooden puppet finger** washed ashore, branded with hell-bounty seal (iron nail through a coin)  
2. **Bottle** with water-damaged line: *“…if the strings reach the sea, cut them before the tide turns…”*  
3. **Black-iron coin** half-buried — later matches Bram’s payment story  

### Private note — Teddy only
> Something in your wrist answers the stone’s pulse. A faint *click*, like a marionette joint. Nobody else notices. You feel watched by the empty air above your shoulders.

### Opening choice (menu, not railroad)
- Stabilize Olive on the sand  
- Study / attune the stone  
- Salvage Longarm / check uncle  
- Walk toward smoke / road / pier  

**Exit Phase 0** the moment someone commits to moving toward town. Don’t hold the beach open.

### Dialog — Uncle (if conscious)
> “We have the heart of the mountain. Don’t drop it. Don’t bargain with it. If it starts singing *names*… tell me which names.”

# PART E — PHASE 1: APPROACH (5–15 min)

One path: dune → cliff stair → outer quay. Optional friction only.

### Encounter: Lyra Quill (fisherwoman)
Hauling nets. Eyes the stone if visible.
> “…That’s humming. Things that hum out here, you throw back.”

**Want:** Someone to believe she saw lantern-lights over open water with no boat.  
**Fear:** Losing her license / being laughed at.  
**Lead:** Lights match Old Tam’s lighthouse stories; also “peddler with too-long fingers” asking about “the girl with sewn eyes.”

They can walk past her. She is a door, not a gate.

# PART F — PHASE 2: TOWN POINTCRAWL (12 NODES)

Give players a **dot map** with names only. All nodes are live.

**How to run:** Ask “Where to?” → run node → when they leave, optionally roll **d20 wander** → next node. If they stall, drop a **proactive node** (Pip with news; child asking about Olive; funeral procession).

## NODE 1 — Salt Row Docks (Knotted Quay)
**Hook:** Fishermen pulled something wrong from the nets after the murders.

**Features**
1. **Widow’s Net** — tangled with real puppet string + carved wooden hand  
2. **Cutter Voss’s chalk ledger** — boat *Merry Widow* marked out, never returned  
3. Gull-picked crate stamped with wax seal: crossed-strings cross  

**NPCs**
- **Cutter Voss** (dockmaster) — Want: clear missing crew’s name. Fear: cargo he let slide is tied to murders.  
- **Lyra Quill** (if not met) — as above.

**Leads out:** Merry Widow course → church cove; lights → lighthouse; peddler rumor → Olive / Watch.

**Complication:** Voss talks freely after **Low Tide Fishing** minigame (or a favor).

**Easter egg:** Carved hand marked **A.W.** = Ambrose Whistlecraft.

**If ignored:** Merry Widow crew becomes background rumor; Voss grows cold toward party.

## NODE 2 — Bram Ferro’s Smithy (Tallow Row)
**Hook:** The blacksmith they promised. Forge half-cold; townsfolk buying arms out of fear.

**Features**
1. Unfinished blades chalked with party measurements  
2. Practice dummy on puppet joints — strings snapped clean  
3. Crate of **black iron** from “upriver” that hums wrong  

**NPC — Bram Ferro**
- Want: finish their order; arm a scared town  
- Fear: he sold child-sized knives to a stranger paid in “coin that wasn’t coin”

**Dialog**
> “You promised you’d come back. I kept the fire lit. Don’t make me melt these into nails.”

**Leads:** Buyer description (tall, too-long fingers, gold thread cuffs); black-iron coin → Mother Yarrow; suggests Watch should know.

**Complication:** Finishing gear = **Temper & Strike** minigame; guard may interrupt demanding lockup chains.

**Easter egg:** Stone near black iron hums the **same two notes** it hums near Teddy.

**If ignored:** Bram sells reserved stock to Watch; later gear is rush-jobbed.

**NPCs**

**Leads:** Uncle visits evenings → Workshop; “man of shadow and string” at window; flinches at church bells.

**Complication:** Real intel from Olive needs **Quiet Game** minigame.

**Easter egg:** Music box lullaby = drunk’s street hum = stone warmth.

**If ignored:** Mid/late session, Olive has wandered toward church alone (gut-punch, not punishment).

# PART I — EIGHT MINIGAMES (drop-in, 5–15 min)

### 1. Low Tide Fishing (Docks)
4 rounds. Each PC narrates help + Athletics/Acrobatics DC 12.  
3–4 success: hand + free talk. 1–2: hand + favor owed. 0: comedy dunking; Voss talks from pity; hand lost.

### 2. Bones & Bells (Tavern)
Hidden 3d6. Claim rising totals or call “Bells!” Deception vs Insight. 3 rounds. Survivor gets true rumor; others get garbled.

### 3. The Quiet Game (Olive)
Gesture code: 1 squeeze=yes, 2=no, tap=wait. Up to 5 yes/no Qs. Insight/Persuasion DC 10 each. 3+ successes: Olive draws sigil or harbor shape unprompted.

### 4. Temper & Strike (Smithy)
3 stages: heat → fold → quench. Athletics/tools DC 13; prior stage adv/disadv. 3 = small perk; 2 = solid; 0–1 = ugly + Bram throws in apology gift.

### 5. Marionette’s Heart (Cobb / Whistlecraft)
3 gates, 3 skills: Sleight (gear), Investigation (thread), Perception (spring), DC 12. Split among party. Clean if <3 total fails; else works with eerie side effect.

### 6. Market Haggle (Square)
3 exchanges. Persuasion/Deception; DC 15 ±2 by RP quality. Beat = discount+info; match = one; miss = spite half-rumor.

### 7. Cold Case (Mortuary/Church)
5 clue cards. Each PC interprets one (Investigation/Medicine DC 12). 3+ correct → “scheduled puppeting that can make mistakes.”

### 8. Resonance Attunement (best at lighthouse)
Perception/Performance DC 14; disadv noisy, adv quiet/water/night. Success: sensory flash (cave memory, muffled voice, color). Max 2 attempts/scene. Fail: stone goes cold, as if it flinched.

# PART J — RESONANCE STONE: TOY, NOT KEY

**Core rule:** It does not answer questions. It **harmonizes with what a place is already trying to remember.**

### Side uses (encourage poking)
| Use | Effect |
|-----|--------|
| **Tune doors** | Tap wood/metal/bone/glass — hear “last note.” Secret panels have no open-history. |
| **Map by sympathy** | Warms near same-source stone (smuggler marks, old foundations, submerged avenue). |
| **Borrow an echo** | Store one sound 1 hour; replay elsewhere; keeps emotional residue. |
| **Quiet a crowd** | Absorbs room rhythm into hush; everyone feels who fights the silence. |
| **Name-sing risk** | If it starts singing names, stop it or note which — Uncle’s warning. |

### Stone reactions cheat sheet
- Near Olive / church residue / black iron / Teddy / Contract tags → warmer / louder  
- Near lighthouse tide bell → clearest  
- If used as spectacle in town meeting → Panic clock +1

# PART K — OPTIONAL UNBINDING (exploratory, not one roll)

Only if they gather pieces and *choose* to try. Binding = braid of **claim + memory + habit**.

### Three strands (any order; partial OK)
1. **Find the claim** — receipt, vow, guestbook note, Cobb’s debt letter, Contract tag logic  
2. **Return a memory** — stone offers fragments from objects; they choose which belongs to Olive  
3. **Teach a new motion** — demonstrate refusal, asking permission, helping, making a mistake (RP montage)

### Concurrent climax scene (if they go for it)
One holds control/steady stone; one gathers witnesses (Bram, Purdy, uncle); one speaks Olive’s name with hope; one decides: burn / return / annotate the “claim.”

### Outcomes
| Result | Fiction |
|--------|---------|
| Full | Eyes + mouth open. Scar-thread only uncle sees. |
| Partial | Eyes OR mouth. One BBEG truth from Olive. |
| Stable-marked | Free, but Puppet Master now has a “favorite seat” in town. |
| Botched rush | Hurt + loosened; next session finishes. |

# PART R — RESEARCH APPENDIX (sources digested)

**Design:** Justin Alexander — node-based scenarios, Three Clue Rule, proactive nodes; Blades-in-the-Dark clocks; Apocalypse World fronts; pointcrawl / Barovia-scale keyed towns; urban faction + rumor systems.

**Puppet theme:** UNIMA “Myth of the Puppet” — marionette vs glove puppet (divine string / earth slapstick); Kleist *On the Marionette Theatre* (grace without self-consciousness); Bunraku (visible manipulators → agency without hidden god); ventriloquism as voice-from-elsewhere; doll-personhood / neglected artificial beings.

**Coastal aftermath:** Ys & Rungholt — drowned bells still heard; low-tide ruins; pride/debt to the sea as competing stories.

**Infernal BBEG:** Contract-devil bureaucracy — letter of law, hidden clauses, collection, receipts, ranking by souls; polite legal language as horror.

**Play pattern:** Soft open → invitations → friction → choice; MacGuffin as curious instrument; social patience pools; minigames that teach later puzzles (temper / tones / consent).
