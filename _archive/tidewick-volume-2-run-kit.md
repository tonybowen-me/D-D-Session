# 2. WHAT THE RESEARCH CHANGED IN HOW YOU RUN IT

| Source | Steal this | Tidewick application |
|--------|------------|----------------------|
| **Curse of Strahd / Vallaki** | Key only ~8–12 locations with payload; dread via liked NPCs; don’t rush the town clock to destruction | Don’t “complete” every cottage. Whistlecraft is warm and likable. Soft clocks, not Vallaki-burn. |
| **Dragon Heist / Alexandrian** | Overlapping faction hooks; interrupt scenes with other scenes; response teams | Mid-interrogation, Pip bursts in with news. Watch / merchants / Olive-circle pull opposite ways. |
| **Phandalin** | Cap spotlight NPCs; promises not map pins; town remembers dungeon deeds | Max 3 “new” named faces per hour. Bram reacts to cave/church. Cobb’s promise = cutter tool. |
| **Ghosts of Saltmarsh** | Town is the spine; player map; private rumor cards; custom downtime | Hand map + 5 private rumors at start. Hinterland: lighthouse, low-tide chapel, sea-cave. |
| **Sly Flourish** | Strong start into a *situation*; 2–3 town features; secrets burn fast in town | Open with one of 3 strong starts (handouts). Tidewick features: strings, salt, debt. |
| **Blades free play** | Free play → committed push → downtime consequences | Exploring = free play. Ritual / evidence trade / Longarm repair = “score.” Consequences after. |
| **Monster of the Week** | 6-step countdown if PCs never intervene; monster motivation one-liner | See §3 below. Ashur’s motivation drives every reaction. |
| **GUMSHOE / Three Clue** | Essential clues free if right place/question; dice for bonus only | Never gate Olive-help or Contract-logic behind one roll. |

**Ashur Vail motivation (MotW-style one-liner):**  
> *To collect outstanding performances with perfect style, and to make the town applaud its own damnation.*

That sentence tells you how he reacts to everything: spectacle > silence, unfinished acts > dead ends, Teddy as understudy > Olive as unfinished lead.

# 3. MYSTERY COUNTDOWN — “If Nobody Stops Him”

Figurative pacing beats. Advance when they stall, waste hours, or fill Panic/Ledger clocks. **Not a script that must occur.**

| Step | Name | What happens offscreen / on arrival |
|------|------|-------------------------------------|
| 1 | **Day** | Town half-open. Doll-bread selling. Watch holds innocent dockhand. Olive stable with Purdy. |
| 2 | **Shadows** | A marionette appears dressed like someone the party *already met*. NOT YET tag moves to a new fountain. |
| 3 | **Sunset** | Public scare: church bell rope twitches during evening prayer; stone shrieks if nearby. Panic +1. |
| 4 | **Dusk** | Watch Captain under pressure frames/hastens trial of dockhand **or** demands party “solve it by morning.” |
| 5 | **Nightfall** | Puppets in Whistlecraft’s shop face *outward* overnight. Guestbook gains a new line naming a PC. |
| 6 | **Midnight** | Soft doom: distant applause; a Contract tag appears on a PC’s gear; Olive walks toward church alone if ignored. |

**Derail freely.** Freeing Olive early jumps to a *different* Midnight (calling card + understudy line). Clearing the dockhand removes Dusk’s scapegoat beat — replace with merchant bribe pressure instead.

# 4. SESSION CADENCE (Blades-adapted)

```
FREE PLAY (open town)  →  SCORE (committed push)  →  BREATH (consequences + bonding)  →  FREE PLAY
```

### Free Play examples
Wandering nodes, tavern games, shopping rumors, Longarm joy-test, Quiet Game with Olive.

### Score examples (declare when they commit)
- Evidence trade with Halloway  
- Temper & Strike finishing weapons  
- Unbinding ritual  
- Clearing dockhand’s name  
- Low-tide drowned chapel delve  
- Breaking into / confronting Whistlecraft after hours  

### Breath (10 min after a Score)
- Tick relevant clocks  
- One NPC reaction to what they did  
- Optional: one player downtime beat (sleep, write letter, tend Olive, clean Longarm)  
- Then reopen Free Play: “Where next?”

**Do not punish mid-conversation.** Heat/suspicion lands in Breath, not while they’re mid-RP.

Beach foam-threads, Longarm hand, stone note, Teddy wrist-click → map.

### B — Situation already running

# 8. EXPANDED TAVERN / STREET MINIGAMES

Use at Drowned Bell, square, or docks. 5–10 minutes each.

### Ship, Captain, Crew (dock dice)
5d6, up to 3 rolls. Must set aside 6 (Ship), then 5 (Captain), then 4 (Crew) in order. Remaining 2 dice = cargo sum. Highest cargo wins a free true rumor from Old Pell; loser buys drinks (flavor).

### Liar’s Dice / Bones & Bells (already in bible)
5d6 hidden. Bid totals. Challenge with Insight vs Deception. Last with dice gets true rumor.

### Arm Wrestling (Bram’s apprentice or dock tough)
Contested Athletics each round; first to 3 net wins. Nat 20 = 2 successes. Winner: free whetstone or dock gossip. Loser: bruise + respect.

### Knife Toss / Tossers
d20+Dex: 1–10 miss, 11–15 outer (2), 16–18 mid (4), 19–20 bull (8). Best of 3. Crowd gathers — Panic −1 if they play friendly; Panic +1 if a thrown knife hits a marionette “for fun.”

### Storytelling Contest
Performance vs rising DC 10/13/16. Beat each = tips. Beat by 10+ = a patron becomes a recurring contact (name them: **Joss Reed**, net-mender).

### Rigged Bug Race (market)
3 racers, hidden d6 each of 3 laps. Scout beforehand (Investigation or 2 sp bribe) to peek one die. Winner: 1d4×10 cp + rumor.

### Puppet-Show Wager (Whistlecraft / square)
Bet which puppet “wins” a scripted duel — pure RP choice. Afterward, passive Perception 15: strings move without the puppeteer’s hands. Seeds plot through play.

### Three Truths of the Cave
Each PC: 2 true, 1 false about the cave. Table guesses the lie. Winner: Inspiration. Loser: stone shows their buried fear privately.

# 9. INVESTIGATION WITHOUT “ROLL INVESTIGATION”

| Technique | Tidewick example |
|-----------|------------------|
| **Gimme clue** | At mortuary looking at wrists → you *see* thread-thin cuts. No roll. |
| **Testimony free** | Yarrow just tells the puppet-from-inside theory if they earned trust. |
| **Player-skill puzzle** | Arrange 5 rumor cards into a timeline on the table. |
| **Fail forward** | Failed Insight on Ambrose → he still points to guestbook, but Panic +1 from raised voice. |
| **Leverage > Persuasion** | Halloway’s fear is wrongful hanging — name that, she opens evidence. |
| **Contact spend** | Bram vouch = Watch listens without checks. |
| **Portable clue** | Missed church tag? Same sigil on dock crate wax. |
| **Living countdown** | Stall → advance Shadows/Sunset for fresh entry. |

# 10. SPLIT PARTY PROTOCOL

- Cap each half-scene at **~5 minutes**  
- Cut mid-tension, not mid-resolution  
- Ripple: Group A tips Watch → Group B hears boots outside tavern  
- Solo secret Teddy beats: between-session note or 60-second private aside, not 20-minute spotlight  
- Reunify via fiction (bells, Olive, Pip) not fiat

# 11. T-30 ENDING MENU (cut clean)

At real-time T-30, pick a shape and steer:

| Shape | Exact cut line |
|-------|----------------|
| **Revelation** | Halloway stares at tags: “There’s a number missing. That one isn’t dead yet.” **Stop.** |
| **Threshold** | Workshop door. Stone humming. Olive’s hand finds yours. “We begin.” **Stop before rolls.** |
| **Dilemma** | Mayor: endorse “bandits” for gold, or tell truth and risk riot. “What do you say?” **Stop before answer.** |
| **Betrayal** | Olive (if mouth free) looks at Teddy: “Does it hurt when they pull?” **Stop.** |
| **Callback** | Applause. Calling card on the anvil. **Stop. Don’t answer Arcana.** |

Say out loud: “That’s where we stop tonight.”

**Pocket emergency cliffhanger:** church bells ring once, off-tempo, from a tower with no one inside.

# 12. SECOND VICTIM BEAT (Mossbank technique)

If Free Play goes long and mystery feels static, during their first full afternoon:

> Pip finds them: “There’s another one. Posed in the square. Hands crossed. And—this one’s wearing a little brass ankle tag. It says NOT YET. Wait. No. It says… a name.”

Use a name of someone they *met* (Lyra, Pell, Fenn) — raised stakes without new NPC overhead. This is optional. Prefer if clocks are stuck at Day.

# 17. BBEG PRESENCE WITHOUT COMBAT (Strahd technique)

Ashur does **not** appear in person this session. He appears as:

- Contract tags  
- Guestbook note  
- Applause  
- Polite posters  
- Marionettes that track Teddy  
- A child selling luck-bread who says his street name  

Players should *feel watched* without a fight. If they demand a confrontation: a reflection bows; a voice from a dummy: “Not yet. The understudy hasn’t learned the part.” Then silence.

# 19. RESEARCH APPENDIX (pass 2)

- Vallaki Companion pacing: introduce characters in small doses; streets as encounter stage  
- Saltmarsh Ch.1: town as campaign focus; player map; downtime between pushes  
- Sly Flourish downtime: warn players ahead; custom location activities; secrets/clues fuel downtime  
- MotW countdown: Day→Midnight as “if ignored”; motivation one-liner; derail proudly  
- Alexandrian: players are not Sherlock; remove chokepoints; GUMSHOE essential clues  
- Pub games: Ship Captain Crew (6-5-4), Liar’s Dice, arm wrestle contests as social glue  
- Mossbank / puppet one-shots: second murder mid-investigation; emotional truth = final clue
