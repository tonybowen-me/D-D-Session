# Minigame Rules: Acts 1–3

Fast, table-ready rules for all structured challenges in `act-1-the-beach.md`, all seven Playable Minigames in `act-2-tidewick.md`, and the Concordance Engine in `act-3-olive.md`.

No game uses combat, attack rolls, enemies, or combat initiative.

## Shared principles

1. Show the goal, tracks, attempts, stakes, and safe exit before rolling.
2. Players choose spotlight order; this is not initiative.
3. Every PC may lead, support, communicate, spend a resource, or protect a track.
4. A player describes what their approach changes; the DM chooses a fitting ability, skill, tool, spellcasting check, or automatic result.
5. Reveal consequences before hard choices and mark visible tracks immediately.
6. Failure adds time, coin, embarrassment, lost clarity, resource cost, or a lesser prize. It never closes the only path.
7. Exact spells/tools can work without a roll but consume their normal resource.
8. Consent is not a check. Social skills clarify or comfort; they do not override bodily autonomy.

## DCs and scaling

Acts 1–2 use their exact printed DCs. Scale Act 3 by average party level, rounded down:

| Average level | Standard | Hard | Desperate |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1–4 | 13 | 15 | 17 |
| 5–8 | 14 | 16 | 18 |
| 9–12 | 15 | 17 | 19 |
| 13–16 | 16 | 18 | 20 |
| 17–20 | 17 | 19 | 21 |

Act 3 party-size scaling:

| Party | Rounds | Resonance cap/round | Adjustment |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 2–3 PCs | 4 | 1 | each PC may lead and make one different support action; release at 5 Resonance |
| 4–6 PCs | 5 | 2 | one action per PC |
| 7+ PCs | 6 | 3 | use 3–4 stations; extra PCs support/mitigate |

## Shared token economy

Use a party pool of up to **6 preparation tokens**. They last through the current act unless stated.

| Token | Spend | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Insight** | before a choice/roll | reveal likely consequence, complication, or useful station |
| **Spare** | after a machine/tool roll | reroll; keep new result |
| **Cadence** | before timing, communication, music, resonance | advantage |
| **Trust** | with affected NPC's consent | turn a mixed success clean, pause safely, or use a listed consent choice |

Gain one token by choosing it instead of progress on a near miss, completing preparation, or spending an apt limited resource. One action creates at most one token. Routine cantrips and Help do not farm tokens.

### Tidewick Favors

Act 2 awards named Favors, but the party can carry at most **3 Favor tokens** into Act 3. Spend one to:

- gain advantage on navigation, tides, rescue, ropes, hulls, contracts, acoustic vibration, or community negotiation;
- produce one packed mundane rescue supply worth 2 gp or less;
- ask for one concrete, truthful craft/environment detail;
- turn a failed noncombat group check into a mixed success;
- contact a friendly Tidewick connection when plausible.

Favors are separate from the six-token preparation pool.

## Check results, Help, and anti-spam

For Act 3:

| Result | Effect |
|---|---|
| Beat DC by 5+ or natural 20 | +2 Resonance, or +1 Resonance and remove 1 Strain |
| Meet DC | +1 Resonance |
| Miss by 1–4 | choose +1 Resonance/+1 Strain, or no Resonance/gain one token |
| Miss by 5+ or natural 1 | +1 Strain and a concrete warning; never instant catastrophe |

**Help:** advantage requires a distinct plausible contribution plus relevant proficiency/feature or a spent resource. The helper shares the consequence. Otherwise Help establishes permission or cancels disadvantage.

**Repeat approaches:**

- first same method/problem: normal;
- second by the same PC: next higher DC (or disadvantage in fixed-DC Acts 1–2);
- third: cannot add progress, only support, mitigate, or reveal information;
- a changed target or problem resets the count.

Athletics to drain Gannet and later brace a flywheel are different. Repeating the same reassurance is not. *Guidance* modifies a roll but is not a contribution itself.

## Act 1: The Beach

### Resonant Hearthstone Investigation

**Time:** 8–10 minutes  
**Goal:** establish Direction, Condition, and Connection  
**Structure:** each useful distinct approach yields one core clue automatically; checks determine precision

**Core clues:** the pulse points inland far beyond Tidewick; it carries living distress, taut twine, obstructed sight, and forced silence; it catches on Pell/Gannet and points to his distant Brackenmill hut.

| Exact DC | Result |
|---:|---|
| 10 | one core clue; effect is present, not an old recording |
| 13 | two clues; pulse worsens slightly |
| 16 | all three plus one vivid nonvisual impression |
| 19+ | ask one limited question about direction, distance, life, worsening, or familiar place |
| 9 or lower | one clue plus harmless sympathetic echo until short rest |

Useful skills: Arcana, Religion, Investigation, Insight, Medicine, Survival, Performance, or relevant tools.

**Automatic experiments:** moving the stone establishes inland pull; nearing Gannet swings its compass; saying Olive's name produces a double pulse. Forceful probing quiets it for ten seconds, then it resumes faster. Magic confirms infernal contract residue, not identity or motive.

**Prize:** all three clues through at least three distinct approaches grants one Insight and one Cadence token for Act 1; record the Brackenmill bearing regardless.

### Stabilize Gannet Nine

**Time:** 6–8 minutes  
**Goal:** drain hip housing, free controls, restore bearing  
**Structure:** one lead check; others support distinctly

| Method | Exact DC |
|---|---:|
| Athletics: shift machine to drain | 11 |
| Sleight of Hand or thieves'/tinker's tools: linkage | 12 |
| Investigation/Arcana: compass oscillation | 12 |
| Perception/Survival: waves and sand | 13 |

Leverage, *shape water*, *mending* on a small break, suitable tools, or an excellent practical plan can automatically succeed.

**Success:** 5 minutes; bearing restored; choose message-lamp or diagnostic lens.  
**Failure:** 10 minutes; Pell sacrifices brass panel or conductive wire; choose one restored system anyway.  
**No action:** Pell stabilizes Gannet in 10 minutes.

**Prize:** restored diagnostic lens grants one Spare at the start of Act 3. Restored message-lamp allows one plausible remote-contact attempt without spending a Tidewick Favor.

## Act 2: Tidewick's Seven Playable Minigames

Each takes 5–12 minutes. Run one by default; run more if time/interest allows. The town remains competent if bypassed.

### 1. Della's Honest Weight

**Attempts:** 3 investigation actions, all distinct  
**Target:** 2 successes; 3 reveals Pip's unrelated altered tally privately

| Action | Exact DC |
|---|---:|
| inspect scale/weights, Investigation | 12 |
| test level/moisture/counter, Perception or tools | 12 |
| calm sellers/reconstruct sequence, Persuasion | 12 |
| improvise counterweight/lever, Strength or artisan's tools | 11 |

**Truth:** damp wood swelled beneath one scale foot; neither seller cheated.  
**Failure forward:** Della refunds both sellers and loses face; party can still repair by labor.  
**Prize:** 2 successes—**Market Favor**, 5 sp provisions, thin-tone rumor. 3—also Pip as guide and delivery map.

### 2. Ropewalk: Keep the Lay

**Track:** Quality 0–3; one PC per stage (or distinct methods if fewer PCs)

| Stage | Exact DC |
|---|---:|
| comb fibers: Athletics, Sleight of Hand, weaver's tools | 11 |
| maintain tension: Constitution (Athletics), Insight, Performance | 12 |
| lay strands: Investigation, Dexterity, artisan's tools | 13 |

Following Ysra's cadence instead of racing grants advantage.  
**Outcome:** Quality 2–3 sound rescue line; 1 Ysra repairs while teaching; 0 decorative cord and kind laughter.  
**Stake:** 2 sp each or one promised labor hour.  
**Prize:** Quality 2—25-foot rescue line and **Ropewalk Favor**. Quality 3—metal filament clue. Quality 0–1—cord bracelet and advantage on next knot check.

### 3. Lead-Line Sounding Chant

**Track:** Course starts 0; reach +2 before −2

| Call | Exact DC |
|---|---:|
| remember marks, History | 11 |
| feel bottom, Survival | 12 |
| chant through noise, Performance | 12 |
| hold current, Athletics | 12 |

Success +1 Course; failure −1. A fitting staff, familiar, peaceful spell, or teamwork can grant advantage.  
**Stake:** losing side buys cider, maximum 1 sp; no one falls in.  
**Prize:** marked sounding line, safe-channel sketch, culvert-depth truth; sketch/lesson grants advantage on one Act 3 navigation/current/depth check.

### 4. Gulls and Tide-Knots

**Track:** 12 buns; three zones

| Zone | Exact DC |
|---|---:|
| Soot Lane awnings | 10 |
| Market Spine crowd | 12 |
| open quay | 13 |

Each zone needs a distinct lead: Acrobatics, Animal Handling, bird-directed theatrical Performance/Intimidation, Nature, Strength shielding, or harmless spell. Success loses none; failure loses 1d3 buns. A funny plan can convert one lost bun into applause.

**Stake:** buns/dignity; replacements 2 cp each.  
**Prize:** deliver 9+—free lunch and **Bakehouse Favor**. All 12—rescuer packets and flue-scrap clue. Fewer—Odo serves survivors anyway.

### 5. Knot-and-Note

**Track:** Harmony 0–3

| Round | Exact DC |
|---|---:|
| pattern: Investigation or Perception | 11 |
| execution: Sleight of Hand, rope knowledge, directing Performance | 12 |
| variation: Insight, Performance, harmless sound/light spellcasting | 13 |

One Harmony per success. Singing, tapping, signing, drawing, or tying all participate fully.  
**Stake:** 3 cp loft tea fund.  
**Prize:** Harmony 2—vibrating-twine observation. Harmony 3—reproducible pitch interval, usable once in Act 3 to set a failed Hearthkeeper roll to Standard DC with no Strain. Harmony 0–1—observation without interval.

### 6. Launch the *Mercy Bell*

**Tracks:** Balance, Momentum, Timing; each starts Neutral and gets one attempt

| Track | Exact DC |
|---|---:|
| Balance: carpenter's tools/Investigation, Perception, Strength | 12 |
| Momentum: Athletics, Constitution, coordinating Performance | 12 |
| Timing: Survival, tide-table Intelligence, wedge Dexterity | 13 |

Success makes the token Favorable; failure Strained. Before launch, sacrifice a personal prize, spend a Favor, or confess a mistake to reset one Strained to Neutral.

**Outcome:** 2+ Favorable—beautiful launch; 1—safe, splashy launch; 0—missed tide and blameless reschedule.  
**Prize:** **Shipwright Favor**, public credit for Kett, half-price yawl. Beautiful launch gives one trained-rescue assist in Act 3 if geographically plausible: remove 1 Strain from one station.

### 7. Hesta's Mussel Court

**Structure:** 3 speakers, one argument each using different evidence  
**Exact DC:** 12 with Persuasion, Insight, History, Investigation, or Performance

Evidence types: worker testimony, harbor finances, and town history. A player may skip a roll by naming a concrete compromise: who pays, who benefits, and how abuse is prevented.

**Stake:** public opinion, never binding law.  
**Prize:** 2 successes—**Council Favor** and Hesta's witness-knot account. Strong compromise—Nym's rescue-fund proposal without exposing Nym. A witness-knot diagram grants advantage on one Act 3 Thread Reader check and authenticates a contract clue.

## Act 3: Concordance Engine Rescue

Use `act-3-olive.md` for safety, narration, Pell dialogue, mystery clues, and aftermath.

### Visible tracks

- **Resonance 0–6** (small party releases at 5)
- **Strain 0–6**
- **Olive's Trust 0–3**, start 1 or 2
- **Rounds:** 4 small / 5 standard / 6 large
- **Catastrophe:** Safe → Warning → Redline → Blackout → Severance

Stable release: Resonance 6 and Strain 3 or lower. Give Olive a communication beat every round; one tap yes, two no, three rapid stop, held squeeze trust.

### Stations

| Station | Approaches | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Hearthkeeper | Arcana, Religion, Performance, instrument | tune Hearthstone |
| Flywheel Engineer | Athletics, tools, *mending* | control Gannet/flywheel |
| Thread Reader | Investigation, Perception, Medicine, divination | find safe sequence |
| Grounder | Survival, Nature, tools, abjuration/energy | bleed feedback |
| Anchor | Insight, Medicine, Persuasion, telepathy | communicate/protect choice |
| Conductor | Performance, History, coordination | synchronize |

### Preparation

One contribution each:

| Approach | DC | Success | Failure forward |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Arcana/Religion read residue | Standard | one clue + Insight | clue, but starting +1 Strain |
| Investigation/Tinker's tools map controls | Standard | Spare | cracked relay appears in round 2 |
| Medicine establish limits/code | Standard | +1 Trust | Pell supplies baseline |
| Insight/Performance/Persuasion rhythm | Standard | +1 Trust or Cadence | Olive corrects rhythm; code still learned |
| craft tools brace station | Hard | prevent first Strain there | flaw revealed; next check there Hard |
| exact creative spell | none/Standard | matching benefit/token | still reveals useful information |

### Round card

Only the first two successful primary actions add Resonance each round (one small, three large). Others remove Strain, create tokens, restore Trust, or cancel complications.

| Round | Pulse | Choice |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Find Her Note | gentle: Standard/max 1 Resonance; fast: resonance advantage/failures +1 extra Strain |
| 2 | False Harmony | spend 1 Resonance for Anchor/Reader advantage; or keep it and Anchor is Hard |
| 3 | Contract Hook | leave it/next Hearthkeeper Hard; burn it/Hearthstone lasting cost +1 Strain; consenting PC shares cost/−1 Strain |
| 4 | Unspooling | Olive chooses protect sight/−1 Strain or restore voice/automatic Standard success/+1 Strain |
| 5 | Release | at most 3 rolls: machine, resonance, communication; Standard at Strain 0–3, Hard at 4–5 |
| 6, large | Stabilize | each station supports; repeated methods cannot add Resonance |

If final Resonance is short by 1: spend 2 Trust with Olive's consent, crack the Hearthstone, or accept partial release.

### Catastrophe card

Strain 6 begins emergency; it does not kill Olive.

| State | Explicit warning and choices |
|---|---|
| Warning, Strain 4–5 | next surge starts emergency; pause for −1 Resonance/−2 Strain or safe abort |
| Redline, Strain 6 | abort; sacrifice equipment; or stabilize: 2 successes before 2 failures at Hard |
| Blackout | Pell states restart can kill; final abort, Pell/Hearthstone sacrifice, or informed consensual restart |
| Restart | 3 successes before 2 failures at Desperate, all distinct; after first failure repeat abort/sacrifice |
| Severance | one final willing sacrifice saves Olive; death only if all decline |

Death requires Strain 6, failed stabilization, chosen restart after explicit warning, two Desperate failures with another warning, and refusal of every final sacrifice. Never one roll.

### Outcome card

| Tier | Requirement/result |
|---|---|
| Perfect | Resonance 6, Strain 0–2, Trust 2–3; all twine removed, apparatus intact, two clues, Counter-Rhythm |
| Clean | Resonance 6, Strain 3–5; all twine removed, two clues |
| Costly | released after emergency/sacrifice; Olive survives, lasting cost, one clue |
| Partial | Resonance 3–5 or safe abort; Olive chooses eyes or mouth freed, one clue |
| Protected failure | Resonance 0–2/early abort; Olive survives, gains Hearthstone communication |
| Death | complete Severance process and all rescues declined |

## Adjudication examples

| Proposal | Ruling |
|---|---|
| “I cast *mending* on the relay.” | exact solution, no roll; resource spent; tool proficiency may add Spare |
| “I use *dispel magic* on twine.” | cannot erase contract; Hard spellcasting check exposes rhythm; failure gives Insight/+1 Strain |
| “I rage and hold the flywheel.” | Athletics, likely advantage; prevents Strain/enables tuning, not direct removal |
| “My familiar watches Olive's taps.” | communication works; Standard Perception may create Cadence |
| “I Persuade Olive to continue.” | no; explain and ask; roll can clarify, never override |
| “I repeat a cold cantrip.” | first normal, second Hard, third cannot add Resonance |
| “We spend three spells on one roll.” | distinct exact effects apply; otherwise one gives advantage and one token maximum |
| “I use an unexpected tool.” | allow if player names what it changes; use listed DC/Standard/Hard |
