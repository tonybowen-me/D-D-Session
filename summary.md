## Canonical setup

- The party recovered the **Resonant Hearthstone** from the coastal cave after a major battle.
- **Pell “Pell” Arkwright**, the Tinkerer, survived. His mecha-machine is **Gannet Nine**.
- Gannet was found on its back in shallow water and needed stabilization.
- **Olive was never in Tidewick.** She remained at Pell's hut in distant **Brackenmill**.
- Olive's eyes and mouth were bound by supernatural puppeteer twine.
- The responsible craft belongs to the **Bailiff of Strings**, a hell-contract bounty hunter and puppet master.
- He took Olive's parents. Their condition after the abduction is not definitively established beyond anything Olive personally witnessed.
- His reason for taking them, employer, current location, and superior contract terms remain unknown.
- Tidewick is a working fishing settlement and potential recurring ally, not a villain lair or disposable quest hub.

## Recommended next-session opening

Start with the strongest unresolved human consequence, not a lore dump.

**If Olive was fully released:** a messenger, sound, or object arrives while Olive chooses breakfast, rest, or whether to inspect a safe fragment.  
**If partially released:** Olive uses her restored sense or voice to make one clear request before the next procedure is discussed.  
**If protected failure:** the Hearthstone carries a deliberate signal from Olive and the party must obtain one missing requirement.  
**If a sacrifice was made:** show its daily cost before offering a way to repair or reinterpret it.  
**If Olive died:** begin with what she changed in the survivors, then let the party choose memorial, accountability, or pursuit; do not reduce her to a revenge trigger.
